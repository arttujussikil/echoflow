# Sentiment benchmark — 2026-10-13

Models label each text as pos / neu / neg with the app's own prompt (`buildLabelPrompt`), 40 texts per request. Accuracy counts unlabelled items as wrong. The 95% interval is a bootstrap over the items: when two intervals overlap a lot, the difference may be noise. Macro-F1 averages F1 over the three labels, so a model can't score well by over-using one label.

## NewsMTSC (sentiment toward a target)

English news sentences about policy issues, each labelled with the sentiment toward one named person in it (5 annotators). 100 per label from the real-world test split. Evaluates the "Mood toward…" feature.

Source: https://github.com/fhamborg/NewsMTSC · Licence: Research use (texts are not redistributed here)

*Hamborg, F., Donnay, K. (2021). NewsMTSC: A Dataset for (Multi-)Target-dependent Sentiment Classification in Political News Articles. EACL.*

300 items (pos 100, neu 100, neg 100).
