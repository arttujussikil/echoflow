How it works

  1. We search everywhere at once

    Reddit, Hacker News, Bluesky, Lemmy, Google News, Yle, Helsingin Sanomat and more, in one go.

  2. The AI reads every post

    Each one is marked positive, neutral or negative, with a few words on why.

  3. You get the mood report

    A summary, the AI's own take, and how the mood differs between sources and changes over time.
